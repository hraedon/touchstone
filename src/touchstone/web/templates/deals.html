Deal feed

Listings priced anomalously low against their own cohort's asking distribution.

A flag here means "cheap relative to what similar listings are asking", not "below market". The reference distribution is built from active asking prices, which skew high, so this is a weaker and more conservative claim than it may look. Being the cheapest in a cohort is arithmetic, not a signal — in any cohort of two or more, something is below p10 — so a listing must also sit at least {{ MIN_SCORE }} spread-unit below the tenth percentile, in a cohort of at least {{ MIN_COHORT_N }}, with a capacity parse that has been trusted or corrected.
A flag also leaves this feed when its listing leaves the market — the same disappearance evidence the trend pages carry, which cannot tell a sale from an ended listing, so retired flags say left the market and nothing stronger. Verify on eBay asks the scanner for ground truth about one listing: one API call, answered on the scanner's next pass.
