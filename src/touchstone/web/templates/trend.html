Disappearances — an inference, not a sales record

How many tracked listings stopped appearing between scans. A listing leaves the active pool when it is bought, when the seller ends or revises it, or when it expires, and the Browse API does not say which. This is drawn as its own chart on its own axis for that reason: it is not comparable with the asking series above, and no price is attached to it anywhere in touchstone. Capped scans contribute nothing here — their disappearances would be an artifact of a moving window.
